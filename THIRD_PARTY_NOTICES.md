## Japanese Google Fonts
Noto Sans JP is bundled unmodified in public/fonts/NotoSansJP.ttf under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; its copyright and license are in public/fonts/OFL-NotoSansJP.txt (distributed as dist/fonts/OFL-NotoSansJP.txt). The Japanese catalogue comes from Google Fonts (snapshot 2026-09-08). Other font files are downloaded from fonts.gstatic.com only when requested; each corresponding copyright and license is stored in electron/font-sources.json and copied next to the user's cached font. Catalogue update: node scripts/update-japanese-fonts.cjs, followed by review of the generated diff and font tests.

Drawing attention sounds (pop, chime, jingle) are original procedurally synthesized PCM generated by Luma Studio. No third-party recordings or sample libraries are used.
